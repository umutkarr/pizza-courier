How to play

  1. Draw one route from the pizzeria through every house. Drag along the streets, or tap a crossing.
  2. Corners cost time. The courier brakes for turns and stops at every house. White dots show where it must slow down.
  3. Deliver and compare with the best possible route. Get within 5% for ★★★.

Privacy

Pizza Courier does not collect any data.

There are no accounts, ads, analytics, trackers or third-party services. The game works fully offline.

Your progress (stars, best times) and settings are stored only on this device. Deleting the app removes them.
