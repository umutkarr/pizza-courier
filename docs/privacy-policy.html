Privacy Policy

Pizza Courier · Effective 4 October 2026

Pizza Courier does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the app stores

Your progress (stars and best times per level) and your settings (sound, haptics, drive speed) are saved only on your device, so that you can continue where you left off. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services or SDKs. It does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and it works fully offline. It makes no network requests.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
