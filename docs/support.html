How do I play?

Draw one route from the pizzeria through every house: drag from the end of your line along the streets, or tap any crossing to extend the line there. Then press the red button and the courier drives your route. The settings button (bottom right) has a "How to play" card.

Why is the shortest route not the fastest?

The courier accelerates, brakes for corners and stops at every house. A route with fewer and gentler turns is often faster even when it is longer. White dots on your line show where the courier has to brake hard.

How do stars work?

Your time is compared with the best route the game can find. Within 5% earns three stars, within 20% two stars, and any completed delivery one star. Finishing a level unlocks the next one.

How do I start over?

Open Settings and tap "Reset progress" twice. This clears all stars and best times on this device.
